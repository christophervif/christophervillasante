About

Where aviation rigor meets digital innovation

I'm a mechanical engineer specializing in aircraft maintenance, modifications, certification, avionics integration and airworthiness support for both rotary- and fixed-wing aircraft.

I lead multidisciplinary projects from concept through certification and implementation — always ensuring compliance with aviation regulations and certification support before the Peruvian Civil Aviation Authority (DGAC). In aerospace, success isn't measured by cost savings but by airworthiness and compliance — and every project I've led has met its certification objective.

Beyond traditional engineering, I design and build custom digital solutions — web applications, databases, API integrations, automation and AI-assisted tools — that turn manual, paper-based workflows into scalable systems. In parallel, I founded Kuranko in 2020, leading international sourcing, supply chain and business strategy.
